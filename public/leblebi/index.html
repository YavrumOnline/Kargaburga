Leblebi

A word puzzle with a real dictionary, in twenty-three languages.

Spell words from a wheel of letters to fill the board, and read the meaning of every word you find. Every puzzle and every definition ships inside the app, so it plays without a connection. A daily puzzle, an optional Google Play Games sign-in that backs your progress up, and nothing to buy.
