Leblebi


Swipe letters, fill the grid, beat the daily puzzle.

get it on google play

HOW IT PLAYS

  • Swipe the letter wheel to spell words
  • Grid words fill the crossword, and extra words score too
  • Stuck? Use a hint to reveal a letter
  • Clear the grid to bank your points and move on

ALMOST 10,000 PUZZLES IN EVERY LANGUAGE

The grids grow as you go, and the words get rarer. There is always another one waiting.

A DAILY PUZZLE

One special puzzle each day. It scores double points.

CALM ENOUGH TO PLAY ANYWHERE

Play to a gentle ambient soundscape: ocean, forest, wind, rain or a distant storm. Each one is generated while you play, so it never loops and never repeats itself. Or turn the sound off and play in silence.

A REAL DICTIONARY

Every word opens in the dictionary with its full meaning. Found a word you don't know? That is the point. Read it and keep going.

MADE TO RESPECT YOU

  • Works offline, with puzzles and definitions on your phone
  • No account, no sign-up
  • No coins, no gems, no shop. Your points are a record, not a currency
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in backs up your progress

TWENTY-FIVE LANGUAGES

Play in Turkish, English, Bulgarian, Catalan, Czech, Danish, Dutch, Filipino, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Icelandic, Indonesian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese (Portugal), Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish or Swedish. Each has its own full dictionary and almost 10,000 puzzles, all included in the app.

Leblebi is free and shows ads. Hints are earned by watching an optional video, and are never required to keep playing.
